Streamlining Banking Support Operations – Business Analysis Agile Project

Prepared By: Ishaan Singh

Project Overview:

This Business Analysis project examines the transition of a global investment bank’s application support operations from an offshore-only model to a hybrid onshore-offshore model. Covering more than 120 applications, the project identifies operational challenges involving fragmented support knowledge, unclear application ownership, escalation processes, and limited visibility. It applies Business Analysis, Business Model Canvas (BMC), process analysis, requirements engineering, solution design, and Agile planning to propose a centralised support operations capability supported by standardised processes and appropriate technology.

Project Components:

1. Original Project Guidelines (PDF):
Outlines the project brief, objectives, required tasks, expected deliverables, and submission requirements for streamlining banking support operations using Agile and Business Model Canvas (BMC).

2. Business Case (PDF):
Defines the business context, problem statement, objectives, solution options, preliminary recommendation, expected benefits, and key risks.

3. As-Is Business Model Canvas (BMC):
Analyses the existing banking support ecosystem across the nine Business Model Canvas building blocks.

4. As-Is Process Map:
Maps the existing support process and highlights challenges involving ownership, coordination, knowledge, and escalation.

5. Fishbone Diagram:
Examines potential root causes contributing to the operational challenges.

6. Stakeholder Map:
Identifies relevant stakeholders and their relationship to the support operations transformation.

7. User Personas:
Represents key users and their needs within the current and proposed support model.

8. To-Be Experience Map:
Maps the proposed support journey from request submission through tracking, investigation, escalation, resolution, and continuous improvement.

9. Business Requirements Document (BRD):
Documents business requirements, stakeholder requirements, functional and non-functional requirements, design considerations, and alignment with the project artefacts.

10. Data Flow Diagram (DFD):
Illustrates the proposed flow of support-related information.

11. User Flow Diagram:
Maps the proposed user journey through the support process.

12. Wireframes:
Presents five proposed interface designs:


1. Request Submission

2. Request Submission Confirmation

3. Request Tracking

4. Support Knowledge

5. Support Overview


13. Jira Agile Project:

Documents the Agile delivery approach and proposed implementation plan through:

1. Jira Epics and User Stories, including acceptance criteria and priorities

2. Jira Sprint Execution Board

3. Jira Sprint Plan

4. Agile Strategy

5. Sprint Plan

6. Release Roadmap

7. Sprint Delivery Matrix

14. Recommendations and Reflection:
Summarises the proposed direction for improving support operations and reflects on how Business Analysis, BMC, and Agile planning support process redesign.

How to Explore:

1. Start with the Original Project Guidelines to understand the assignment brief and expected deliverables.

2. Review the Business Case to understand the business problem, objectives, and solution options.

3. Explore the As-Is BMC, Process Map, Fishbone Diagram, and Stakeholder Map to understand the existing operating model and identified challenges.

4. Review the Personas and To-Be Experience Map to understand stakeholder needs and the proposed future-state support journey.

5. Examine the BRD, Data Flow Diagram, User Flow Diagram, and Wireframes to understand the documented requirements and proposed process and interface designs.

6. Explore the Jira Agile Project to review the epics, user stories, sprint planning, Agile strategy, release roadmap, and sprint delivery matrix.

7. Finish with the Recommendations and Reflection to understand the proposed direction and the application of Business Analysis and Agile principles.

Note: The project presents a proposed operating model and supporting analysis, requirements, designs, and Agile plans. The centralised support operations capability is a preliminary recommendation, not a confirmed or implemented solution.
